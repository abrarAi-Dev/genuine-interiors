WHAT WE DO

Our Services

Everything for your home, under one roof - from the first foundation stone to the final coat of paint.

Residential Construction

Complete home and villa construction - foundation, RCC, masonry and finishing, with quality checks at every stage.

Interior Design & Execution

Living rooms, bedrooms, pooja units and full-home interiors, designed around your family's way of living.

Modular Kitchen

Kitchen layouts planned properly before work begins - plumbing, granite or quartz countertops and smart storage.

Glass & Metal Works

Glass roofing, shower enclosures, partitions, glass doors, and staircase and balcony railings.

Doors, Windows & Aluminium

Strong, neat doors, windows and aluminium works, made and fitted to the approved design.

Flooring, Tiles & Stone

Flooring, wall tiles, granite and stone works with clean levels and smooth finishing.

Waterproofing & Anti-Termite

Protection for bathrooms, terraces and the whole home, with pressure and leakage testing.

False Ceiling & Painting

False ceilings with planned lighting, and smooth, long-lasting paint finishes inside and outside.

Plumbing & Electrical

Plumbing, sanitary, electrical and lighting works - installed carefully and tested before closing.

Façade & Landscaping

Façade, external works and landscaping that give your home a beautiful final look.
